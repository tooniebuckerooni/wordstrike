How to play WordJab

WordJab is a free multiplayer word battle game — part hangman, part knockout round. Every player hides a secret word, then you take turns throwing letters at each other's boards. Guess right and you keep jabbing; crack someone's whole word and they're out.

  1. Host a game and share the 4-letter code, or gather around one screen with pass-and-play.
  2. Pick your secret word — a real word or phrase up to the character limit. Nobody else can see it.
  3. Take turns jabbing. Pick an opponent, throw a letter. A hit reveals every matching tile (blanks come one at a time) and you jab again; a miss passes the turn.
  4. Win the fight. In Last Standing, survive with your word uncracked. In Points mode, score for every hit and cracked board.

Too busy for a full bout? The Daily Jab gives you one word and six jabs every day — keep your streak alive and share your result.

FAQ

Is WordJab free to play?

Yes — completely free. No download, no sign-up, no account. Open wordjab.io in any browser and start playing.

How do I play with friends online?

One player hosts and gets a 4-letter code. Everyone else taps Join a game and enters the code. Works on phones, tablets, and computers.

How many players can play?

2 or more — online with a game code or pass-and-play on one device. It shines as a party game with 3 to 6 players.

What is the Daily Jab?

A free solo challenge: one secret word, six wrong guesses allowed, one new word every day. Build a streak and share your score.

Do I need to install an app?

No. WordJab runs entirely in your browser. On mobile you can add it to your home screen for an app-like experience.
